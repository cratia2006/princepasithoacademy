About Prince Pasitho Academy

Located in the heart of Nyali near Ratna Square, we provide a foundation of excellence for children aged 3 months to 9 years. Under the leadership of Madam Agnes Wasai, our institution is dedicated to nurturing young minds in a safe, stimulating, and modern environment.

We believe that every child is a unique star. Our mission is to provide holistic education that balances academic achievement with physical, social, and emotional growth.

Our Academic Programs

Day Care & Playgroup

A home away from home for babies from 3 months old. We focus on sensory play, nutrition, and early social milestones.
